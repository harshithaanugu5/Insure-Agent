# Coverage Policy

## General underwriting rules
- Applicants under age 25 require additional verification.
- Coverage above $750,000 requires enhanced review.
- Smoking status must be disclosed and may affect pricing.
- Medical disclosures should be evaluated with due diligence.

## Eligibility requirements
- Age must fall within valid underwriting limits.
- Minimum annual income must be documented when coverage exceeds a standard threshold.
- Supporting documentation may be requested for medical conditions.
